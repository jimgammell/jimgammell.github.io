## About

I'm a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at
Purdue University. My doctoral research focuses on deep learning
interpretability for hardware security.

My broader interests include explainable AI, LLMs and agentic
systems, and embodied AI.

Previously, I've interned at Amazon, Sandia National Laboratories,
the German Aerospace Center, and NIST.
